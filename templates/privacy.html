Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

This Privacy Policy explains what information the {{ brand }} service ("the Service", operated by {{ author }}) collects, how it is used, and your choices. We take a deliberately minimal approach: the Service exists to publish our own educational content to accounts you authorize, and it collects only what is needed to do that.

1. Information we access

  • Basic profile information — when you connect a TikTok account (scope user.info.basic), we receive basic profile details such as your open ID, display name, and avatar. We use these only to confirm which account is connected and to display it back to you.
  • Publishing permission — the scopes video.upload and video.publish allow the Service to upload and post videos that we supply to your authorized account. We do not read, download, or analyze your existing posts, followers, messages, or private data.
  • Access tokens — we store the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform so the Service can post on the schedule you set, without asking you to log in each time.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect information about the people who watch or interact with the published videos.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • The public website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

3. How we use information

Information is used solely to: (a) authenticate the connection to your account; (b) upload and publish the educational videos you have chosen to that account; and (c) keep a record of which videos have been posted so the same clip is not published twice. We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and a minimal posting record are stored on a private server we control, protected by the hosting provider's access controls. Tokens are used only to communicate with the corresponding platform's official API over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

5. Data sharing

We share information only with the platform you authorized (for example, TikTok), and only as required to publish your content through its official API. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep access tokens only for as long as your account remains connected. You can disconnect at any time from your platform's "connected apps" / "app management" settings, which revokes our access. To have any stored tokens or posting records deleted from our side, email {{ contact }} and we will delete them promptly.

7. Third-party platforms

When the Service posts to a platform, that platform handles the resulting content and any viewer data under its own privacy policy — for example, TikTok's Privacy Policy. This policy covers only the Service itself.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults managing their own content and educational accounts. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: {{ contact }}.
